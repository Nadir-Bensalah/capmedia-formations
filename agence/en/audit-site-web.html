Audit my website.

Your site is live, but you can tell it is not working for you: slow, invisible on Google, or visited without ever producing a single client. We examine the whole thing and tell you exactly what to fix, in what order, and why.

What we examine

Five angles. Nothing more, nothing less.

01

Speed

Real load times, on mobile and on an average connection. Every extra second costs you visitors, and Google measures it too.

02

Technical SEO

Tags, structure, sitemap, structured data, duplicate pages, indexing errors: everything that keeps Google from understanding you.

03

Mobile

More than half your visitors are on a phone. We check that your site reads well, navigates well and converts there, not just that it displays.

04

Basic security

Properly configured HTTPS, protected forms, up-to-date versions, security headers. The bare minimum that prevents lazy hacks and the "Not secure" warning.

05

Conversion

Can a visitor tell within 5 seconds what you do, and what to do next? We follow the whole journey, from the first screen to the contact.

What you get

A written, prioritized report, in plain English.

Not a 60-page automated export with color-coded scores. A written document that lists every issue found with three pieces of information: what it is, what it costs you, and how to fix it.

  • Issues ranked by impact: you know where to start
  • For each point, the fix explained step by step
  • Screenshots that show the problem, not just describe it
  • An estimate of the fix time for each point

And then, two paths

You apply it yourself. The report is written for that: every fix is actionable by you or by your current provider. No dependence on us.

Or you hand us the fixes. We price the list, you choose what we fix, and the cost of the audit is deducted if you give us the job.

The audit is useful even if we never speak again: that is by design.

Who it is for

It is for you if…

  • Your site has been live for months and brings in no clients
  • You pay a provider and want an independent opinion on their work
  • You are about to invest in ads and want to check the site holds up before sending traffic to it
  • Your site was built years ago and you are torn between fixing it and rebuilding it
  • Google cannot find you, even when you search your own name

How it works

Three steps, zero pointless meetings.

Step 1

You write to us

Your site's address, what you expect from it, and what frustrates you. You get a precise quote quickly, no surprises. Nothing starts before you agree.

Step 2

We examine

We run the site through the five angles, by hand and with our tools. No access to your hosting is needed for the audit.

Step 3

You get the report

The written, prioritized document, within 7 working days, with an email exchange for all your questions. Then you decide what comes next.

Questions

Before you decide.

›My site runs on WordPress, Wix or Shopify: does that work too?

Yes. The audit covers what your visitors and Google see, not the technology underneath. The recommendations take into account what your platform actually lets you fix.

›Are you going to tell me everything needs rebuilding?

Only if it is true, and that is rare. Most sites can be fixed. If rebuilding costs less than fixing, the report says so with the numbers to back it up, and you remain free to do it with whoever you want.

›Do you need access to my site?

No, not for the audit: everything is observed from the outside, like a visitor. Access only comes into play if you later hand us the fixes.

›How much does it cost?

It depends on the size of the site: auditing 5 pages and 300 pages is not the same job. Request your quote, quick reply with a precise price, no surprises.
