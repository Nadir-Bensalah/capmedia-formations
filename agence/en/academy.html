Why the academy exists

Our view of this job fits in one sentence: if you can do it yourself, we would rather teach you than bill you.

A typical agency benefits from making everything look complicated. We build and publish apps every day, and we know that a good part of the journey is within reach of a motivated person with proper guidance. The academy is that guide: the whole route, in the right order, with the traps flagged before you fall into them. And the day your project outgrows what you want to do alone, the agency is here.

How it works

A path, not a pile of courses.

01

8 steps to a published app

The main path takes you from zero to your app live on the stores, one step at a time, in the right order. No abstract theory: every step produces something concrete.

No running subscription, no modules sold piece by piece: a single pack unlocks the rest of the path, and it is yours for good.

The apps built by the academy's members, on display. The best proof that the path leads somewhere is the apps of the people who followed it.

Taught by

Taught by the people who build.

The courses are written by the Capmedia team: the same developers who build the apps and websites of the agency's clients, and who submit their own apps to Apple's and Google's review. What we show you, we did last week, not in another life.

The same standard on both sides. The pre-submission review included in the path is the one we sell to the agency's clients on the pre-submission testing page. It is not a lighter version for students.
