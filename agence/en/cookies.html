Privacy

Cookies and data

This site is deliberately plain: no advertising trackers, no third-party analytics, nothing sold to anyone.

What this site stores

One thing only, on your device: your theme choice (light, dark or automatic) and your answer to the consent banner, so it stops asking for six months. Nothing is sent to a third party, nothing identifies you.

What this site does not do

No Google Analytics, no advertising pixel, no social widget that follows you, no data shared with ad networks. This page exists to say it plainly, not to tick a box.

If you request a quote

What you enter (name, email, phone, company, project description) is used only to answer you and prepare your proposal. It is kept for the duration of the conversation, never resold, and you can ask for its deletion at any time at contact@capmedia.app.

You can reopen your preferences anytime: . Clearing this site's data in your browser also clears them.
