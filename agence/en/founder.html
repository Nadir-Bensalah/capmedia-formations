The founder

Nadir Ben Salah.

Independent mobile app developer, founder of Capmedia Digital and Capmedia Academy. He is the person behind the agency's projects, from the first exchange to the release.

The background

A self-taught developer, shaped by the field rather than the lecture hall.

Nadir learned the craft by building: real projects, real mistakes, and the discipline of going all the way each time, right up to the release. AI has been part of his daily tools for years, not as a showcase argument but as working leverage: it accelerates what he already knows how to do, it does not replace it.

What he builds

Four apps in preparation for the stores.

Alongside client projects, Nadir is preparing his own apps for the App Store and Google Play. That is what feeds the agency: he faces the stores' rules for himself first.

MindDrop

Capture an idea by voice before it slips away, and let the app file it on its own: notes, tasks, reminders, calendar.

Qindil

A calm spiritual companion: reading, listening and daily anchors, with a carefully crafted visual identity.

Flowi

Watch your income in real time, second by second, to regain a concrete feel for what you earn.

Isogonic

A precision calculation tool designed for a demanding profession, built to work entirely offline.

The stack

Tools chosen, not collected.

React Native and Expo to build once and publish on iPhone and Android. Firebase for the server foundations. Stripe for payments. A tight stack, mastered in depth, the same for his apps and for client projects.

The conviction

Ship rather than prototype.

A prototype sleeping in a drawer teaches nobody anything. Everything Nadir does aims at a real release: Apple's review, the Google Play listing, the first users. And what he practices, he teaches: that is why he founded Capmedia Academy, where the courses follow exactly the method used for clients.
