The audit · Mobile app

Audit my mobile app.

Your app is live, or almost, but something is off: users who leave, middling ratings, sluggish screens, or just a nagging doubt. We put it under the microscope with the eye of a team that ships its own apps, and you walk away with an action plan, not a list of blame.

What we examine

Four angles, from the first screen to the data.

01

User experience

The full journey, from first launch to daily use: the onboarding that drives people away or keeps them, the screens where they get lost, the iOS and Android conventions your app breaks and that your users feel without being able to name.

02

Performance

Startup time, list smoothness, battery and data consumption, offline behavior, binary size. We measure on real phones, not just on a simulator running on a brand-new Mac.

03

Store compliance

Apple's and Google's rules change without warning: mandatory in-app purchases, account deletion, permissions, listings and screenshots. We spot what could get you rejected at your next update, before it happens.

04

Data security

Exposed API keys, database rules left wide open, personal data traveling in the clear, a privacy questionnaire that contradicts reality. The mistakes we find in every other app, and that cost dearly the day someone discovers them.

What you get

A prioritized action plan, not a trial.

Every finding is ranked by two simple questions: what is it costing you today, and what does it cost to fix. The result is an ordered list: what is urgent, what pays off, what can wait.

  • Critical fixes first: security and rejection risks
  • Quick wins next: big effect, small effort
  • Every point explained: the problem, the evidence, the fix
  • An email exchange after you read it, for all your questions

The angle that changes everything

The audit is done by a team that ships. Not a firm applying a checklist: developers who submit their own apps to Apple's and Google's review, and who know what passes, what breaks, and what makes people uninstall.

Then, as with all our audits: you apply it yourself, you hand the fixes to your team, or you hand them to us. The report is useful in all three cases.

Who it is for

It is for you if…

  • Your app is live but people install it and then disappear
  • You had your app built for you and want an independent opinion on what was delivered
  • Your ratings are stuck and the reviews mention slowness or crashes
  • You are planning a rebuild and want to know what deserves to be kept
  • You store client data and have never had its security checked

How it works

Three steps, as always.

Step 1

You write to us

A link to your app (or TestFlight access), what worries you, and access to the code if you want the deep security angle. A precise quote, no surprises, sent quickly.

Step 2

We test

On real iOS and Android devices, in real conditions. With the code if you provided it, without it otherwise: the audit adapts to what you can share.

Step 3

You get the plan

The written, prioritized action plan, within 7 working days, followed by an email exchange. You or your team can apply it directly.

Questions

Before you decide.

›Do I have to give you the source code?

No, it is not required. Without the code, we audit everything that can be observed from the app itself, and that is already a lot. With the code, the security and performance angles go deeper. Either way, an NDA gets signed without discussion if you ask for one.

›My app is not built with React Native: is that a problem?

No. UX, perceived performance, store compliance and data hygiene can be audited whatever the technology. If your app is in Swift, Kotlin or Flutter, the source code analysis will simply go less deep than with React Native, and the quote will say so honestly.

›What if the audit finds nothing serious?

Good, and it happens. You walk away with written confirmation that the foundations are sound, plus a list of improvements ranked by gain. Knowing your app is solid has value too, especially before you invest in acquisition.

›My app is not published yet: is this the right audit?

If it is finished and your main goal is avoiding a store rejection, look at Test my app before submission instead: that offer is built for that exact moment. The full audit is broader and deeper.
