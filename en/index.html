What actually blocks people

  • The $99/year Apple developer account, and its enrollment that fails
  • Certificates, app identifiers, provisioning profiles
  • The first upload that fails with an unreadable error
  • Apple's rejection after 48 hours, three cryptic lines long
  • Screenshots in 6.9", 6.5", 13" iPad formats
  • The mandatory privacy policy, and where to host it
  • App Store Connect's privacy questionnaire
  • The paywall, in-app purchases, subscriptions, taxes
  • App Store search: the right title, the right keywords
  • Google Play, its mandatory 12 testers, its delays

Each of these points has made thousands of people give up. Each one is a chapter of this course, written by someone who hit the wall before you.

Questions

What everyone asks.

›I am a complete beginner. Like, truly.

That is exactly who this is for. Module 2 explains what a terminal is and where to click, screenshot by screenshot. If you can install software and follow instructions in order, you qualify.

›Do I need a Mac?

No. Builds happen in the cloud with EAS, so you can publish to the App Store from Windows. You will need a phone to test on: yours is enough.

›Is this no-code?

No, on purpose. No-code locks you in: you pay a subscription forever and you do not own your app. Here you produce real code that you own; the AI just writes it under your direction.

›Which technology exactly?

React Native with Expo: one codebase for iPhone and Android. It is what Discord, Shopify and Coinbase use, and it is what AI models know best, which matters when they write the code.

›What if Apple rejects my app?

It happens to professionals too. Module 14 lists the 11 most common rejection reasons with, for each one, the fix and the exact message to send the reviewer. A rejection is a round-trip, not an ending.

›What language is the course in?

The course content is currently written in French. This page and the member area interface exist in English, and an English version of the full course is on the roadmap. If you read French comfortably, everything works today.

›Will I make money?

Nobody can promise you that, and you should distrust anyone who does. What you get for certain: a published app and the skill to build the next one in two weeks instead of six months. The math section above gives you the honest numbers.
