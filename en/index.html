What actually blocks people

  • The $99/year Apple developer account, and its enrollment that fails
  • Certificates, app identifiers, provisioning profiles
  • The first upload that fails with an unreadable error
  • Apple's rejection after 48 hours, three cryptic lines long
  • Screenshots in 6.9", 6.5", 13" iPad formats
  • The mandatory privacy policy, and where to host it
  • App Store Connect's privacy questionnaire
  • The paywall, in-app purchases, subscriptions, taxes
  • App Store search: the right title, the right keywords
  • Google Play, its mandatory 12 testers, its delays

Each of these points has made thousands of people give up. Each one is a chapter of this course, written by someone who hit the wall before you.
