Claude Code: The Developer in Your Terminal

The tool professional developers use to delegate code. Setup, method, guardrails: everything that separates the toy from the production tool.

Start for free See the curriculum

The problem

You've tried getting an AI to code in the browser: it works for five minutes, then it forgets your project, invents files, and has you copy-pasting fifty times an hour.

Claude Code is a different category: it lives in your terminal, sees your real files, edits them, runs your tests, fixes, tries again. It's a tireless junior developer, provided you know how to direct it.

This course teaches you to direct it: clean installation, context file, permissions, subagents, hooks, and the working method that produces reliable code instead of mush.

This is for you if

  • You've tasted vibe coding and want to go professional
  • You're building a product and browser AI is wasting your time
  • You're a developer and want to multiply your output without sacrificing quality
  • You finished the mobile course and want to industrialize your method

Questions

Before you decide.

›Do I already need to know how to code?

No, but you have to be willing to learn to read code. The course teaches you to direct and to review, not to write.

›Is it expensive to run?

An entire module is devoted to costs: understanding the billing, choosing your plan, and the habits that divide your consumption by three.

›Why Claude Code and not another tool?

Because it's the most capable terminal tool on the market, and the one the author uses in production every day. The method carries over to its competitors.

›Is it really free?

Yes: this course is a free step of The App Developer Path. Every module is open, no credit card is asked for. If you enjoy the path, the next steps unlock with the pack.

›How do I access the course?

Create your account with your email, no password: the course unlocks immediately, in full. All your courses live in one place, under one email.
